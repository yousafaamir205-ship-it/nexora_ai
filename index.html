What's actually different here

Most platforms sell you videos. We built NEXORA around the parts of learning to code that videos can't do.

Mentor-led, not video-only

Every week you get live time with an engineer who reviews your code the way their own team would — comments, pushback, and all.

Graded like a real PR

Projects are reviewed for readability and edge cases, not just whether the output matches. You'll get comments you'd actually get at work.

A cohort that sticks around

Your Slack channel doesn't close on graduation day. Most alumni are still in it, still answering questions, two years on.

Three tracks, one format

Pick the track that matches where you want to work. The mentorship model is the same across all three.

Frontend Engineering

Semantic HTML, component architecture, and the CSS layout systems that hold real products together.

Backend & Databases

API design, query performance, and the normalization habits that keep a schema from turning into a mess.

Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Access control, threat modelling, and secure-by-default habits for people who'll be shipping code, not just auditing it.
